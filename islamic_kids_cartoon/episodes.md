# Nanha Ahmad aur Dost — Islamic Kids Cartoon (3 Episodes)

**Format:** har episode 3–4 minute, 3D/2D animation, bachon (3–8 saal) ke liye. Zubaan: Urdu.

## Characters
- **Ahmad** (6 saal) — shararati magar dil ka achha larka
- **Maryam** (5 saal) — Ahmad ki choti behan, sawal bohat poochti hai
- **Dadi Ammi** — meethi, samajhdar, kahaniyan sunati hain
- **Mishu** — pyari billi (bolti nahi, sirf "meow")

**Theme song (10 sec):** "Ahmad aur Maryam, seekhein har din kuch naya, Allah ka naam lekar, karein sab achha!"

---

## Episode 1: "Bismillah ki Barkat"
**Sabaq:** Har kaam Bismillah se shuru karna.

1. **Subah, kitchen.** Ahmad bhooka hai, bina kuch kahe jaldi se paratha khane lagta hai. Maryam: "Bhaiya, Bismillah?" Ahmad: "Baad mein!"
2. Ahmad ka paratha haath se phisal kar Mishu ke paas gir jata hai. Mishu bhaag jati hai, doodh ka glass ulat jata hai.
3. **Dadi Ammi** aati hain, muskura kar samjhati hain: "Bismillah kehne se Allah ki barkat shamil hoti hai, aur shaitan hamare khane mein shareek nahi hota."
4. Ahmad sharminda hota hai. Naya paratha lekar kehta hai: **"Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem"** — is baar sab kuch achha hota hai, Mishu ko bhi thoda doodh milta hai.
5. **Ending:** Ahmad camera ko dekh kar: "Dosto, aap bhi khane se pehle Bismillah kehna!" Gaana + dua: *"Allahumma barik lana feema razaqtana"*.

---

## Episode 2: "Sach ka Meetha Phal"
**Sabaq:** Hamesha sach bolna.

1. Ahmad football khelte hue Dadi Ammi ka pyara phool-daan tod deta hai. Dar ke mare chupa deta hai.
2. Dadi Ammi poochti hain: "Beta, phool-daan kisne toda?" Ahmad kehta hai: "Mishu ne!" Bechari Mishu ko daant padti hai, wo udaas ho jati hai.
3. Raat ko Ahmad ko neend nahi aati. Mishu ki udaas aankhein yaad aati hain. Maryam kehti hai: "Nabi ﷺ ne farmaya: sach neki ki taraf le jata hai."
4. Ahmad subah Dadi Ammi ke paas jaa kar sach bata deta hai aur maafi mangta hai.
5. Dadi Ammi gale lagati hain: "Sach bolne wale ko Allah pasand karta hai." Dono mil kar naya phool-daan sajate hain. Mishu khush.
6. **Ending:** Ahmad: "Sach bolo, dil halka rahega!" Gaana.

---

## Episode 3: "Baant kar Khana"
**Sabaq:** Sharing aur padosiyon ka khayal rakhna.

1. Eid ka din! Dadi Ammi ne bohat saari sewaiyan banai hain. Ahmad aur Maryam naye kapre pehen kar khush.
2. Maryam dekhti hai ke padosi **Bilal** ek taraf akela baitha hai, uske ghar mein Eid ki tayyari nahi.
3. Ahmad pehle sab sewaiyan khud rakhna chahta hai. Dadi Ammi: "Nabi ﷺ ne farmaya: wo shakhs momin nahi jo khud pet bhar kar khaye aur uska padosi bhooka rahe."
4. Ahmad ek bara katora sewaiyon ka bharta hai, Maryam ke saath Bilal ke ghar jaate hain. Bilal ki ammi aankhon mein aansu ke saath dua deti hain.
5. Sab bachay mil kar Eid manate hain, Mishu bhi naya ribbon pehne nazar aati hai.
6. **Ending:** Ahmad: "Baantne se khushi barhti hai!" Eid Mubarak gaana.

---

## Production notes
- Har episode ke end mein ek chhoti dua/hadees on-screen likhi aaye (Urdu + Arabic).
- Nabi ﷺ ya Sahaba ka chehra/animation kabhi na dikhaya jaye; unka naam adab se (ﷺ / رضی اللہ عنہ).
- Music: sirf daff/vocal rakhein agar aapki audience ki taraf se tarjeeh ho.
