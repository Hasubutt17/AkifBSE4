# Episode 3: "Baant kar Khana" — Google Flow Prompts

Google Flow mein har prompt ek alag clip (8 sec) banata hai. Total 15 clips = ~2 minute.

## Step 1: Style lock (har prompt ke shuru mein paste karein)
> Cute 3D Pixar-style kids animation, warm soft lighting, bright festive colors, family friendly. Characters: Ahmad (6-year-old boy, big brown eyes, white shalwar kameez, small embroidered cap), Maryam (5-year-old girl, pink frock, white dupatta), Dadi Ammi (kind elderly grandmother, light green shawl, glasses), Bilal (6-year-old neighbor boy, simple faded kurta, sad), Mishu (small white cat with a red ribbon). Pakistani home, Eid decorations, crescent moon lanterns. No text on screen.

Tip: Flow mein pehle Ahmad, Maryam, Dadi Ammi, Bilal, Mishu ki ek-ek image bana kar "Ingredients" mein add karein taake chehre har clip mein ek jaise rahein.

## Clips (har ek 8 sec, 16:9)

1. **Intro:** Wide shot, Eid morning, sunlight through window, crescent-moon lanterns hanging. Narrator (cheerful female voice): "Eid ka din tha, aur Ahmad ka ghar khushiyon se bhara hua tha."
2. **Sewaiyan:** Dadi Ammi sets a huge bowl of sewaiyan on the table, steam rising. Dadi Ammi: "Aao bachon, Eid ki sewaiyan tayyar hain!"
3. **Naye kapre:** Ahmad and Maryam twirl in new clothes, giggling. Maryam: "Bhaiya, hum kitne pyare lag rahe hain!"
4. **Mishu:** Mishu with a new red ribbon rubs against Maryam's legs. Maryam: "Mishu ko bhi Eid mubarak!"
5. **Bilal akela:** Through the window Maryam sees Bilal sitting alone on his doorstep, no festive decoration. Maryam softly: "Bhaiya, Bilal akela baitha hai."
6. **Ahmad ki lalach:** Ahmad hugs the sewaiyan bowl close, hesitating. Ahmad: "Yeh sab meri hain!" (playful, then thoughtful face).
7. **Dadi ki baat:** Dadi Ammi kneels, gentle smile. Dadi Ammi: "Nabi ﷺ ne farmaya, wo momin nahi jo khud pet bhar kar khaye aur uska padosi bhooka rahe."
8. **Dil badalna:** Ahmad looks at the bowl, then at Bilal through the window, smiles. Ahmad: "Dadi Ammi, hum Bilal ko bhi sewaiyan dein?"
9. **Katora:** Ahmad and Dadi Ammi fill a big decorated bowl with sewaiyan, Maryam adds dry fruits. Dadi Ammi: "Shabash, mere bachon!"
10. **Raasta:** Ahmad and Maryam carry the bowl carefully across the street, Mishu follows. Maryam: "Bismillah, sambhal ke bhaiya!"
11. **Bilal ke ghar:** Ahmad holds out the bowl to Bilal at his door. Ahmad: "Bilal, Eid Mubarak! Yeh tumhare liye."
12. **Bilal ki ammi:** Bilal's mother, eyes tearful, hands raised in dua. Bilal's mother: "Allah tumhein hamesha khush rakhe, beta."
13. **Mil kar Eid:** All kids sit together eating sewaiyan, laughing, Mishu with ribbon in the middle. Bilal: "Yeh sabse achhi Eid hai!"
14. **Ending message:** Close-up of Ahmad smiling to camera. Ahmad: "Dosto, baantne se khushi barhti hai!"
15. **Outro:** Wide festive shot, all characters wave. Cheerful kids song voice: "Eid Mubarak, Eid Mubarak, sab ko Eid Mubarak!"

## Step 3: Jorna
Flow ke "Scenebuilder" mein 15 clips ko order mein jorein, ya CapCut/DaVinci mein join karein. Urdu dialogues clear na aayein to subtitle add kar lein.
